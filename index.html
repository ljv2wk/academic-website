Lauren Van De Hey

Hello! I am an Assistant Professor in the Political Science program within the School of Social Sciences at Utah State University.

As a scholar, I research social and political health identities, their creation, public opinion surrounding them, the political psychology of stigmatized health identities, and their public policy implications. I study these concepts within American politics using experimental, survey, and qualitative interview methods.

I graduated with my Ph.D. studying American politics at the University of Virginia. My dissertation research was supervised by Nick Winter, Paul Freedman, Justin H. Kirkland, Kirill Zhirkov, and Ashley Jardina and focused on the intersection of mental health, political identity, and political behavior.

Prior to studying at UVA, I earned a B.A. in political science (with high departmental honors) and a B.S. in general biology from the University of California, San Diego.

My broader research interests include public opinion, political psychology, health politics and policy, race and ethnic politics, survey research methods, and item development.
