Teaching and mentoring

Teaching

I teach courses in American politics, political psychology, public opinion, and political identities. My classes connect foundational political science research with contemporary political questions and the methods used to investigate them.

Approach

Teaching with evidence

Across courses, I emphasize close engagement with research, productive discussion of disagreement, and careful evaluation of evidence.

My interests in survey research and measurement also inform my teaching and mentoring, particularly around the design and interpretation of survey questions and experiments.
